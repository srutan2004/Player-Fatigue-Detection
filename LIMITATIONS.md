# Measurement and interpretation limits

- **No fatigue ground truth:** Work rate/projected gait describe movement. Tactics, opportunity, pacing, injury and position can cause similar changes. Trajectories and substitutions are contextual proxies, not fatigue labels. Phase 4 is implemented, but no onset passes all evidence checks. No substitutions are verified for this cohort.
- **Identity assistance:** The principal study uses supplied identities/pitch annotations to constrain detected-person association. Coordinates remain detector-derived, but annotation agreement after this gate is conditional. It cannot establish independent tracking accuracy. A separate no-hint MOT audit is provided.
- **Reference/color uncertainty:** Audited pitch annotations use coarse coordinate grids. Kit colors are supporting evidence and are unreliable under shadows/stripes; ambiguous palettes remain unknown. Visible-tracklet continuity is bounded to 0.8 s and explicitly tagged. The archived raw study tracker used a shorter occlusion horizon than the corrected fresh-extraction default; fragmentation remains a limitation.
- **Pose noise/selection:** Small bodies, blur, occlusion and crowding degrade joints even when confidence is high. Native crops/larger pretrained pose improve availability, not missing image detail. Only suitable observed straight running contributes gait, leaving many windows unavailable.
- **2D projection:** Knee angles are projected; stride length is ground path per cycle; vertical oscillation is a dimensionless image proxy. Elevated hips cannot be mapped through a ground homography to obtain physical vertical meters. Oscillation peaks are not verified foot strikes.
- **Calibration/camera motion:** Curved panoramas failed a single homography. A rational warp passed withheld reference-point checks, but these sparse points do not establish player-position accuracy everywhere. Outside-hull mapping is unavailable. The fixed-camera guard flags pan/zoom rather than recalibrating; unavailable registration remains unverified.
- **Temporal noise:** Detector box-bottom jitter and tracklet changes affect speeds/bout availability. Smoothing stays within valid sequences, 40-ms propagation is tagged, and ground displacement never bridges missing observations. Joints are never interpolated. Turn/overlap checks cannot classify all jumps/tackles.
- **Labels and evaluation:** Training combines MOT boxes and visually reviewed model-assisted staff proposals. Original box alignment was sampled rather than every box reannotated; tiny background people may remain unlabeled. This can bias detection AP. The separate MOT audit measures known-player recall/consecutive switches, not complete all-person precision or IDF1/HOTA.
- **Comparability:** Speed/body-scale/image-motion strata reduce confounding but do not recover true body facing or remove perspective/acceleration/context effects. Windowed gait requires enough accepted exposure; an unavailable estimate is intentional.
- **Small study:** Three paired players/two matches support a descriptive case study. Overlapping display windows are dependent; non-overlapping windows are separate. Population-level statistical claims or causal fatigue claims are unsupported.
- **Clock:** GSR/MOT indices and PTS origin are handled explicitly. The configured 45-minute second-half offset omits first-half stoppage time. Unprocessed intervals between early/late clips cannot reveal exact onset.
- **Execution:** One detector adaptation was completed and the baseline retained. Concurrent GPU workers caused one CUDA error; partial attempts were excluded and later GPU stages ran serially. Runtime/VRAM measurements depend on source, model, hardware and settings.
- **Actual inferential availability:** 63 accepted same-leg cycles yield zero eligible 120-second gait windows. These cycles support descriptive audits only. The first ten elapsed minutes lack sufficient qualified baseline exposure for A_blue_36 and B_white_17. Normalized trends stay unavailable instead of moving the baseline later or lowering thresholds.
- **Selective observation and small paired sample:** B_white_17 and B_white_9 late periods fail coverage/count gates; averaging their few surviving observations can bias comparisons. Only A_blue_36 qualifies for an early/late movement pair. The configured paired test requires at least three players and is not run here. The periods are selected clips rather than complete halves.
- **Statistics and sensitivity:** Lag-1 HAC and multiplicity adjustment on tiny time series are descriptive approximations, not reliable clinical confidence. Smoothing/window/penalty checks reduce fragility but do not validate physiological fatigue or account for every analytic choice. Synthetic known-change/null controls validate software behavior only.
- **Broadcast stress audit:** Five public SoccerNet-GSR test clips were sampled at approximately 2 fps. Recall concerns labeled players/goalkeepers only, not every person. Pose confidence from 100 selected large matched-player crops is availability, not accuracy or an unbiased pose benchmark. Static-camera checks mostly fail; dynamic broadcast calibration is not implemented. Short clips are not stitched or used for long-term decline claims.
